Delete your account

Wedding Guest Camera — Trove · by Getsnippet

You can permanently delete your Trove account and its data at any time, directly from inside the app. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Wedding Guest Camera (Trove) and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.
  4. Your account and data are erased immediately.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently erases:

  • Your profile — name, profile photo, and any socials you added.
  • The films (events) you host, and every photo and frame in them.
  • Your participation records in other films.
  • Your purchase records associated with the account.

Deletion is immediate and permanent — it cannot be undone. Content that other members captured in a film you did not host stays with that film. Routine backups and logs are cleared within 30 days. We may retain a minimal record only where required by law (for example, a record of a purchase for tax purposes).

Delete specific data (without deleting your account)

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove data. Inside the app you can:

  • Delete an event (film) — open the film, go to Film settings → Delete film. This removes the film and every photo and frame in it.
  • Delete a single photo — open the photo, then ⋯ → Delete.
  • Edit or remove profile details — change or clear your name, profile photo, and socials in Settings.

These changes take effect immediately. To request removal of specific data you can't reach yourself, email trove@getsnippet.co from your account's email address; we respond within 30 days.

Can't access the app?

If you can no longer sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Email trove@getsnippet.co

Please send your request from the email associated with your account so we can verify it. We respond to deletion requests within 30 days.
